**SPHERE Research and Advocacy** is looking for a passionate and dedicated individual to join our team as a **Program Officer (PO)**. In addition to core programmatic responsibilities, this role will also be appointed in charge of administrative duties.

If you have a background in human rights, social sciences, or development work and are eager to bridge analytical policy research with grassroots community mobilization, we want to hear from you!

### 📋 Position Overview

- **Position:** Program Officer (PO)

- **Reporting Structure:** Reports directly to the Executive Director

- **Commitment & Office Hours:** Part-time (minimum 80 hours per month at the initial stage). Compulsory office presence of 3 days per week (including Monday and Tuesday) at SPHERE's Colombo office, with extensive outstation travel.

- **Recruitment Status:** Open Call (open to qualified external candidates)

### 🔍 Key Responsibilities

#### 1. Programmatic Mandates (Program Officer)

- **Project Cycle Management:** Plan, execute, monitor, and evaluate programmatic activities (PMEL) in strict accordance with project timelines, budget allocations, and donor agreements.

- **Community Mobilisation & Network Building:** Identify, mobilize, and maintain relationships with diverse grassroots community networks, young activists, and marginalized groups across target provinces.

- **Workshop Design & Facilitation:** Design training modules, develop educational curricula, and facilitate interactive workshops, focus group discussions, and multi-stakeholder consultations.

- **Monitoring & Narrative Reporting:** Systematically collect programmatic data (pre/post tests, attendance registries, feedback forms) and draft high-quality narrative progress reports for donors.

- **Advocacy & Campaigns:** Coordinate regional capacity-building workshops, field research (including RTI filings and policy documentation), and multilingual digital multimedia campaigns.

#### 2. Administrative Mandates (Administrative Oversight)

- **Office Administration & HR Support:** Manage daily office routines, maintain centralized digital/physical filing systems, verify staff timesheets, and track leave requests.

- **Financial Upkeep & Bookkeeping:** Record daily transactions, manage petty cash, issue reimbursement payments, file receipts, scan invoices, and maintain standard Expense and General Ledgers.

- **Logistics & Compliance:** Coordinate secure travel logistics (including trackable rideshares), manage event/workshop arrangements, organize audit preparation folders, and handle digital/social channel updates.

### 🎓 Qualifications & Experience

- **Educational Background:** Bachelor's degree in Development Studies, Sociology, Human Rights, Political Science, Communications, Law, or a related social sciences field.

- **Professional Experience:** Minimum of 2 years of active experience coordinating projects, facilitating training, or conducting field research within the Sri Lankan non-profit or activist sector.

- **Language Skills:** High-level professional fluency in English, with Sinhala and Tamil trilingual skills considered a major, decisive advantage.

- **Core Competencies:** Proven experience filing RTI requests, executing qualitative/quantitative surveys, synthesizing findings into policy briefs, and managing basic digital content creation or media production.

### How to Apply

Eligible candidates are invited to submit their **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** along with a **Brief Expression of Interest** outlining their suitability for the role to [**contact@sphereresearch.org**](mailto:contact@sphereresearch.org).
